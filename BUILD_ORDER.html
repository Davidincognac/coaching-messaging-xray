Phase 1

Foundations

Settled. Nothing here blocks the social page any more.

One visual language, decided

partly built

The ground tells them whose page it is. Dark navy when we are talking: the triggers report and the sales page. Light when we are showing them their own material: the social report and the website report. Dark, light, light, dark.

Type is already uniform. Both pages use Inter and SourceSerif, so what is left is the ground and one shared set of components: cards, quote blocks, scored bars, section headers. The audit already has most of them.

There is a practical reason as well as a felt one. A website screenshot and a photograph of a person are light objects and sit badly on navy, which is why the audit already puts them on light cards inside a dark shell.

Extend the leads table

built

Seven columns added: the banner path, the bio, the last post, the website, and a timestamp for each step a coach finishes. Added by migration, so the leads already in there kept their place. update_trigger_lead() only ever adds and ignores an unknown column, so a typo cannot corrupt a row, and mark_step() stamps social, website or sales.

This is what lets the sales page know what evidence it holds, and what makes drop-off visible in phase 6.

Where uploaded images live

built

storage.UPLOADS_DIR follows the same rule as the database: Render's persistent disk when deployed there, the project directory locally, and a loud warning with a fallback if the disk is missing. Without it every banner a coach uploads would vanish at the next deploy.

Phase 3

Social page

Built. The form, the upload, the token, the report and both exits all run, and the chain from the triggers page through to the exits has been followed end to end.

File upload

built

The app's form handler reads plain text only, capped at 64KB. Accepting an image means multipart parsing, a size cap, and checking the file really is an image rather than trusting what it claims to be. This is the one genuinely new piece of engineering and the one real security surface.

Done: banner_image.py takes the raw bytes and hands back something safe to store. It rejects anything that is not an image, caps the upload at 10MB, applies the rotation a phone records so a sideways photo comes out the right way up, and drops all EXIF, which matters because a phone photo can carry the location it was taken at. Filenames come from the lead token, so one coach cannot overwrite another's. A /uploads/ route serves them back, and it only serves files matching the names we write. Tested against nine real coach banners plus empty files, oversized files, a PDF, an HTML page renamed to .png, and path traversal in the token.

Settled: stored as WebP quality 80, longest edge 1600px. Those nine banners went from 14.7MB to 584KB, about thirty times smaller, worst single file 165KB. WebP beat JPEG roughly two to one on every one. Checked by eye at magnification on the hardest cases, fine gold linework on dark purple, a serif over a pink gradient, condensed caps with a tiny trademark symbol, and a sunset photo. No visible difference from the original. 1600 rather than 1400 because a phone upload arrives portrait, and it is the width that carries the text.

The parsing, now done: Python 3.13 removed cgi.FieldStorage, so multipart.py is our own, with a cap on every axis a stranger controls: body size refused on Content-Length before a byte is allocated, part count, per-part header size, per-field size, one file only. Repeated field names keep the first value, so nothing can be smuggled past a check that only read one. 39 adversarial tests in test_multipart.py.

The form

built

Banner, bio, last post, and the website as an optional fourth. No name, no email, no platform. At least one field filled, and the page survives every combination.

Decide: this list had the website as a separate step and you later floated putting it on the same page as an optional field. It is built the second way, with the two exits kept for anyone who leaves it blank, so both readings work. Say if you want it off the form.

Phase 5

Sales page

756 lines of finished sales copy, personalised from the coach's own audit. The work here is making it survive a coach who has no website, not rewriting it.

Everything the page does today

built

Seven sections built around their own record: their score, their headline, their screenshot, the four-part diagnosis, the false beliefs, the reveal and the checkout. Already reskinned once and copy-reviewed. One page that adapts, rather than two versions that drift.

Recognise the token, not just a domain

not started

It identifies people only by their website domain. A coach with no website has no domain, so today the page cannot greet them, cannot show a score, and cannot show any of their own evidence. This is what breaks the shortcut from the social page.

Gate every section on the evidence held

not started

Score, screenshot and headline appear only for a coach who gave a website. Everyone gets the argument built on their triggers. No section ever renders with a hole in it, and nothing claims evidence we do not have.

Use the triggers, do not repeat them

not started

They have already read them and already accepted them. The argument is the distance between what their market buys on and what their page scored. Re-explaining the triggers re-sells something already agreed, which weakens it.

Phase 6

Across every page

The things that go wrong quietly, and only show up with a real coach's data.

Run the copy checker on every new page

partly built

check_copy.py renders the real pages and fails on banned words, missing contractions, advice aimed at the coach, dead links, empty sections and unrendered template text. It needs pointing at the social and sales pages as they are built.

Now carries the full house style blocklist as well: the fancy register, the buzzwords, and the shapes. It flags a banned JOB rather than a banned word, and the market's own language is masked before anything is tested, so a coach's own vocabulary is never touched.

The social pages are covered now, in both their states, empty form and filled-in report. Running it found one real contraction miss in copy a coach reads, and two faults in the checker itself: it was treating the stylesheet and our own HTML comments as copy, so it reported words that are nowhere on the screen.

Still uncovered: the sales page, and /combined in its three shapes. Worth knowing while you are there: the sales page has two “this is not about” constructions that break the contractions rule. They are deliberate-looking emphasis in copy you have already signed off, so they are flagged rather than changed.

Settled: US spellings are fine. The checker no longer flags them and the cleaner no longer rewrites them. The list stays in clean_banned.py as a comment, one line to switch back.

Hunt the claim-without-checking bug on every page

not started

Four of the five bugs found today were the same fault: copy asserting something without checking it was true of that coach. We trimmed your post when we had not. Read your banner when there was no banner. Section 5 of 5 with one section on the page. Every new page needs testing against a coach who gave the bare minimum.

Mobile

not started

A pasted bio, a wide banner and a long post are all things that break a narrow screen. Check nothing scrolls sideways.

Delete uploaded photos after 30 days

not started

Long enough that a coach coming back at the weekend finds their report whole, short enough that you are not warehousing photographs of people. Storage is not the reason: a banner is about 2MB, so a thousand coaches is 2GB.

A sweep when the app starts, plus a check whenever a social report renders. No scheduler to forget. What happens afterwards is already built and tested, because the social section survives a missing banner and simply stops mentioning one.

One line under the upload field, as a fact rather than a warning: we keep your screenshot for 30 days so you can come back to this report, then we delete it. The report itself is kept, because it is a few kilobytes and it is the thing that sells.

See where people drop out

not started

Recording which steps each token completed costs almost nothing once the leads table is extended, and it is the only way to know whether the social page earns its place.

Markets we do not hold

not started

The photo deletion note

not started

Say it as housekeeping, not as a deadline to act on. Your house style rules out fake scarcity, and a real deletion used as a lever still reads like one.
