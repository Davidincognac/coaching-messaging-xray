To start a health or wellness coaching business, pick one health goal for one group of people, build one package with a price, stay clearly on the coaching side of the medical line, write a one-page plan with the client sum on it, and go where that group already asks for help. The training is the easy bit. The business side is where health coaches get stuck.

![Two women walking and talking along a park path on a sunny autumn morning](how-to-start-a-health-coaching-business.jpg)

This is about the business. If you're still deciding whether to train, [how to become a health coach](/blog/how-to-become-a-health-coach) covers that.

## Things to know

- "Health coaching" is too broad to sell. Pick one problem, like getting back to fitness after having a baby.
- Health coaches support behaviour change. They don't diagnose, treat or prescribe.
- Wellness coaching is the same business with a wider brief: sleep, stress, energy, balance.
- Out of 10,954 coaching websites we scored, 85.7% fail a five-second test of "who is this for?". Health sites are no different.
- A one-page plan tells you how many clients you need before you spend money.

## Step 1: pick one health problem for one group

"I help people get healthier" sells nothing, because nobody wakes up wanting to get "healthier". They wake up wanting to stop feeling exhausted by 3pm, or to get their blood sugar down before their next check-up, or to fit back into their work trousers.

So pick one. A made-up example: "I help office workers in their 40s who've put on weight since working from home get back into a routine." You can find those people. You know what they moan about.

The health and wellness markets are crowded. In 47.7% of the coaching markets we mapped, buyers have already seen so many promises that a plain "lose weight" claim doesn't get through. Being specific is how you get noticed. [How to choose a coaching niche](/blog/how-to-choose-a-coaching-niche) helps you narrow it.

## Step 2: know where the medical line is

This is the step health coaches can't skip. Health coaching is about helping someone change habits and stick to a plan. Diagnosing, treating conditions or prescribing diets for a medical condition is medical work.

In practice:

- **Coaching:** "Let's find a walking routine you'll actually keep."
- **Not coaching:** "You've got insulin resistance, so cut out these foods."

If a client has a medical condition, the plan comes from their doctor and you help them stick to it. Write that into your contract. [Does a life coach need a business license](/blog/does-a-life-coach-need-a-business-license) covers the wider rules, and there's a [coaching contract template](/blog/coaching-contract-template) with the wording.

## Step 3: build one package

One offer with a start and an end. A made-up example: "12 weeks back on track", with six video sessions, a weekly check-in message and a simple plan at the end.

Health changes take time, so short packages often stop before habits stick. Ten to twelve weeks is a common length for that reason. [The coaching offer](/blog/coaching-offer) goes into how to build it, and [coaching pricing](/blog/coaching-pricing) helps you set the number.

## Step 4: write a one-page plan

Do the sum. Income you want, plus costs, divided by your price, gives you the number of clients you need. If your package is £600 and you want £24,000 a year with £2,000 of costs, you need about 44 clients a year. That's nearly 4 a month.

If that number looks impossible, change the price or the package now, on paper. The [coaching business plan](/blog/coaching-business-plan) shows the whole thing filled in.

## Step 5: set up the basics

- Register the business where you live.
- Get professional liability insurance, and tell the insurer it's health coaching.
- A way to book a call and a way to take payment.
- A simple page that says who you help, what changes for them and what to do next.

## Step 6: find your first clients

Go where your group already is. New mums are in local parent groups. Office workers are on LinkedIn and in work wellbeing schemes. People who've just had a health scare are in condition-specific forums and support groups (be careful there, and don't sell to people mid-crisis).

Have real conversations. Twenty of them will teach you how your buyers describe their problem. Use their words on your page, not "holistic wellness".

If you want to see what health coach websites get right and wrong, we scored a batch of them in [health coach websites, scored](/blog/health-coach-websites-scored).

## Is a wellness coaching business any different?

Only in the brief. Wellness coaching covers a wider spread, like sleep, stress, energy and work-life balance. The business steps are the same. The risk is that "wellness" is even vaguer than "health", so step 1 is even more important. Pick one: "sleep for shift workers" beats "wellness for busy people" every time.

## How does this fit with starting any coaching business?

Health coaching is a coaching business with one extra rule, the medical line. ## FAQ

### How do I start a health coaching business?

Pick one health goal for one group of people, build one package with a price, stay on the coaching side of the medical line, write a one-page plan with the client sum, set up the basics (registration, insurance, booking, a simple page) and go where your group already asks for help.

### Do I need a certification to start a health coaching business?

Not legally in the UK or the US, because health coaching isn't licensed. Training helps you coach well and some buyers look for it. It doesn't let you diagnose or treat. That stays with licensed professionals.

### How much does it cost to start a health coaching business?

It can be done cheaply. Training is usually the biggest cost. After that you need insurance, a booking and payment tool, and a simple website or page. Running costs can be well under a couple of hundred a month.

### What's the difference between a health coach and a wellness coach?

A health coach usually works on specific health goals, like weight, activity or sticking to a doctor's plan. A wellness coach covers a wider area, such as sleep, stress and balance. The business setup is the same for both.
