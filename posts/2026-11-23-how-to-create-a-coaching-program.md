To create a coaching program, start with the result your client wants and work backwards. Write the 3 to 6 steps it takes to get there, decide how long it runs and how you'll deliver it, give it a name a buyer understands, and put a price on it. A signature coaching program is that same thing, the one main program you're known for.

![A woman leaning over a wooden table laying out a row of handwritten index cards in order](how-to-create-a-coaching-program.jpg)

## Things to know

- Start from the result, not from what you like teaching.
- A program has a start, an end and a clear result. "Ongoing coaching" isn't a program.
- 3 to 6 steps is enough. More than that and people lose track.
- Name it in your buyer's words. Clever names make people work to understand you.
- Out of 10,954 coaching websites we scored, 82.3% show no price at all. Put the price on the program.

## Step 1: write the result in one sentence

What will your client have at the end that they don't have now? Write it the way they'd say it.

A made-up example: "You'll have a plan for your first year of retirement and you'll know what to do with your days."

Not "You'll step into your next chapter with clarity." That's coaching talk. Nobody searches for it and nobody can picture it.

If you can't write the result in one plain sentence, go back to your buyers. Twenty conversations will give you their words. [The coaching offer](/blog/coaching-offer) helps here too.

## Step 2: map the steps to get there

Think about the people you've coached. What did they have to get through, in what order? Write those as 3 to 6 steps.

The made-up retirement coach might have:

1. Work out what you actually miss about work
2. Sort out the days, so they have a shape
3. Agree how you and your partner share the house now
4. Pick one or two things to work towards
5. Write the year-one plan

Each step becomes a session or a week. If you've not coached many people yet, your steps will change as you go. That's fine. Run it, then fix it.

## Step 3: pick a length and a format

Match the length to the change. Small habit changes might take 6 weeks. Bigger changes, like a career move, often need 3 months or more.

Then pick the format:

- **One-to-one:** the most attention and the highest price.
- **Group:** several people at once, cheaper per person. [Group coaching programs](/blog/group-coaching-program) covers running one.
- **A mix:** group sessions plus a couple of one-to-one calls.

Add what goes between sessions: a message check-in, worksheets, a short recording. Keep it light. Extras nobody uses don't add value, they add work.

## Step 4: name it plainly

"Your first year of retirement" tells people what it is. "The Phoenix Rising Method" doesn't. A plain name also means people can recommend you without having to explain the name.

## Step 5: price it

Work out the price from your client sum: the income you want, plus costs, divided by the number of clients you can coach in a year. Then check it against what the result is worth to the buyer. [Coaching pricing](/blog/coaching-pricing) goes into the detail. A payment plan makes a bigger price easier to say yes to.

## What is a signature coaching program?

It's your one main program. The thing people know you for and the thing you sell more than anything else. You don't need a special template. It's the steps above, done properly and refined as you coach more people.

Having one main program helps buyers too. Out of 10,954 coaching websites we scored, 85.7% fail a five-second test of "who is this for and what does it fix?". A page selling one clear program is far easier to understand than one selling six.

## How do you know it's working?

Watch three things: do people finish it, do they get the result, and do they tell others? If people drop out at the same step, that step needs fixing. If they get the result, ask for a testimonial right then, because buyers in 92.9% of the coaching markets we mapped decide on what other people say.

## FAQ

### How do I create a coaching program?

Write the result your client wants in one sentence. List the 3 to 6 steps to get there. Pick a length and a format (one-to-one, group or a mix). Give it a plain name and a price, then run it and improve it.

### How long should a coaching program be?

Long enough for the change to happen. Habit changes might take 6 weeks, bigger life or career changes often 3 months or more. Match the length to the result.

### What is a signature coaching program?

It's the one main program a coach is known for and sells more than anything else. It's built the same way as any program, from the result back to the steps, and improved over time.

### How much should I charge for a coaching program?

Start from your client sum: income wanted plus costs, divided by how many clients you can take. Then check it against what the result is worth to your buyer.
