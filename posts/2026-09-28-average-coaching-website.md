We scored 11,011 coaching homepages out of 10. The average came in at 4.5.

That is not a story about lazy coaches. Every one of those sites was built by somebody who knows their subject.

## Where the marks go

The scoring splits into eight checks. Most sites lose the same one first: whether a stranger understands what you do inside five seconds.

83% fail that check.

## Why the same gap keeps appearing

A coach writes their homepage from the inside, in the words they use with other coaches. Their buyer is not a coach.

## FAQ

### What does the five-second test actually measure?

Whether a stranger can say what you do and who you do it for, from your homepage alone, in five seconds. 83% of the 10,954 sites we scored could not pass it.

### Is a low score a problem with my coaching?

No. The scoring only reads your homepage. It says nothing about how good you are in the room, and the two are not related.

### Why does the average sit at 4.5 and not lower?

Most sites do the basics. They load, they use HTTPS, they have a contact route. The marks are lost on clarity and on whose language the page is written in.
